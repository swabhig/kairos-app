# verbe.store 🗣️
> *content into conversation — questions into clarity*

paste any article, blog post, or newsletter URL — and just ask questions instead of reading everything.

**live → [verbe.store](https://verbe.store)**

---

## what it does

- paste a single article or substack/newsletter archive URL
- it parses the content
- you chat with it — answers grounded in the source
- conversations are saved

---

## how this started

i read lenny's newsletter obsessively. watching him build lennybot — first as an MVP, now on [delphi.ai](https://delphi.ai) — made me think: what if you could *talk* to the people who shaped how you think, not just read them?

verbe is step one of that.

---

## what's next

- **digital avatars of leaders & mentors** — a lennybot-style AI trained on everything your favourite founder or thought leader has written and said.
- **RSS-based newsletter extraction** — smarter ingestion of substack publications, with paywall detection built in.
